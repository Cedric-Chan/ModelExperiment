# Impeccable — design context

Persistent guidelines for UI work in this repository. **Primary scope:** `docs/prototype/model-experiment-web/`.

## Design Context

### Users

- **Product:** **Aimos** is the external-facing data AIO platform; **Model** is a submodule within it.
- **Roles:** Algorithm engineers, data scientists, and strategy users who configure offline training, monitor runs, and manage artifacts/builds (see root `README.md` and `docs/design/`).
- **Job to be done:** Move from ad-hoc Python orchestration to **form- and canvas-driven** training configuration, execution on Ray, and traceable outcomes—without unnecessary UI noise.

### Brand Personality

- **Tone:** **Engineering-first**—precise, trustworthy, and utilitarian; avoid marketing gloss or “flashy” product chrome.
- **Naming in UI:** Prefer **Aimos** as the platform brand and **Model** (or clear submodule labeling) where the submodule is in scope; keep copy aligned with that hierarchy.
- **Reference feel:** **Dify**-like products are a good benchmark: clear hierarchy, calm surfaces, tool-like density—not decorative or experimental motion for its own sake.

### Aesthetic Direction

- **Theme:** **Light mode only** for the intended product surface. Do not treat dark mode as a first-class requirement; avoid designs that assume or prioritize dark backgrounds.
- **Palette & chrome:** Align with the **online feature platform** direction documented in the repo: primary **`#13c2c2`**, neutral page background **`#f0f2f5`**, text around **`#333`**, borders **`#e8e8e8`**—as already expressed in `model-experiment-web` tokens (`theme.css`, `AppShell`).
- **Typography:** **DM Sans** for UI, **IBM Plex Mono** where monospace helps (logs, IDs, code-like fields)—per `fonts.css`.
- **Anti-patterns:** Overly “炫酷” visuals, heavy gradients-as-hero, or playful illustration-led layouts that compete with operational content.

### Design Principles

1. **Clarity over spectacle:** Favor readable tables, forms, and status semantics; motion and decoration only when they aid scanning or feedback (Dify-like restraint).
2. **Aimos-aligned color system:** Keep primary teal and neutral grays consistent; do not introduce competing brand colors without explicit product decision.
3. **Light-first surfaces:** Design, test, and ship for **light** backgrounds and contrast; skip dark-mode polish unless product scope changes.
4. **Prototype scope:** These rules **primarily govern `docs/prototype/model-experiment-web`**. The root README may describe a future Ant Design Pro shell; still keep this prototype coherent with the above until a separate design handoff says otherwise.
5. **Sensible defaults:** Prefer conventional admin/SaaS patterns (side nav, dense but legible lists, obvious primary actions) over novel interaction experiments.
